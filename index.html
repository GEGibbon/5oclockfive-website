About

Pueblo's local source for live entertainment

The 5 O’Clock Five is a professional jazz combo comprised of Colorado State University Pueblo students and alumni, delivering live music tailored to each event. Featuring saxophone, piano, guitar, bass, drums, and percussion, the group performs a diverse repertoire spanning jazz standards, swing, Latin, funk, fusion, and other contemporary favorites.

Whether providing sophisticated background music for cocktail hours and receptions or engaging audiences with high-energy performances, The 5 O’Clock Five brings exceptional musicianship, professionalism, and versatility to every occasion.
